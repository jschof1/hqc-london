## Review rules applying to every page

The exact proposed SEO title, meta description and single H1 of each page were compared with its v2.1 URL and keyword owner. They are suitable **except where an amendment is named below**. At implementation, render the document's main section labels as H2 and their subordinate labels as H3; numbered process steps do not automatically need heading tags. The copy does not justify extra H1s, duplicated service pages or a new keyword owner. Use lowercase, trailing-slash, non-www HTTPS canonical URLs and link directly to them, never through a legacy redirect.

For service pages, use `Service`, `WebPage` and `BreadcrumbList` only for the visibly described service; keep the matrix's page-specific exceptions below. Structured data must describe visible, verified facts. A proposed schema type is not a claim of search-result eligibility. Keep home, property, workplace and complex primary intents distinct. In particular, `/deep-cleaning-london/` owns residential deep-clean intent while `/commercial-deep-cleaning-london/` owns workplace/site deep-clean intent; `/move-in-cleaning-london/` addresses private clients while `/move-in-void-property-cleaning-london/` addresses professional property teams. Internal links can bridge those journeys without merging their keywords.

Every enquiry CTA must land on the appropriate buyer/service state of `/request-a-quote/` or an assessment conversation. An enquiry or suitability submission is not a booking. **Only an eligible Essential Clean™ visitor may proceed to direct booking after the approved suitability and provider hand-off exist.** Image uploads, GHL fields, Automaid/Launch27, Stripe, consent, tracking and proof are later dependencies, not live promises. Proof placeholders and named reviews/logos/images must not be published without evidence and permission.
