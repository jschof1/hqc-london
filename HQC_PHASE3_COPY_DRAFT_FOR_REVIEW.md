# HQC Phase 3 Copy Draft for Review

Date: 20 May 2026

Purpose: give Pedro a reviewable first copy draft, backed by SEO/CRO reasoning, before implementation.

## Strategic Copy Position

High Quality Clean should be presented as a practical, premium cleaning partner, not a vague luxury cleaning brand.

Recommended positioning line:

> Cleaning support for London homes, property teams, and premium spaces that need to stay ready.

Why this works:

- It names the work clearly: cleaning.
- It names the markets: homes, property teams, premium spaces.
- It turns the benefit into a practical outcome: spaces stay ready.
- It keeps the premium signal through the audience and standard, not through decorative language.
- It supports the new three-segment strategy.

Avoid leading with:

> Where profound care meets perfected detail

Reason:
It sounds elegant, but it does not quickly tell a cold visitor what HQC does, who it is for, or why they should enquire.

## Why We Wrote It This Way

This section is not a package comparison. It is a decision shortcut. Competitors such as Maid2Clean reduce friction by asking users to self-identify early. Total Clean and CCM do the same through service and sector clarity. This section brings that discipline into HQC’s premium positioning.

The order is deliberate:

1. Property and Facilities first because the Drive doc identifies it as the largest revenue opportunity and currently underrepresented on the site.
2. Homes second because it is the current revenue base and should remain prominent.
3. Commercial and Wellness third because it has margin potential but should not dominate until proof is stronger.

# 5. SEO/CRO Rationale

## SEO Decisions

- Titles include service, location, and brand because Google recommends clear, concise titles that accurately describe the page.
- Meta descriptions are written as one-to-two sentence page summaries because snippets influence click choice.
- H1s use plain category language rather than poetic positioning.
- The homepage uses broad category language, while hub pages use more specific service and buyer language.
- The new structure supports service-plus-location expansion later, especially End of Tenancy Wandsworth, Office Cleaning Belgravia, and Post-Renovation Cleaning London.

## CRO Decisions

- The hero answers what HQC does, who it helps, and what action to take.
- The three-segment section reduces thinking effort by letting users self-select.
- Pain sections make the copy more relevant to real buyer frustration.
- Trust strips appear early because cleaning has access, damage, and reliability concerns.
- CTAs say what the user gets: quote, property support, home cleaning quote.

## Competitive Logic

- Total Clean shows the value of operational trust and clear commercial service language.
- Complete Clean Management shows the value of pain-led copy and sector clarity.
- Maid2Clean shows the value of simple self-selection and direct next steps.
- Specialist property-cleaning competitors show that property buyers respond to phrases like void, post-tenancy, handover, managed properties, inspection-ready, and fast turnaround.
