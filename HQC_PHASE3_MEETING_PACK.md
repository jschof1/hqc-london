# HQC Phase 3 Meeting Pack

Date: 21 May 2026

Purpose: a meeting-friendly version of the Phase 3 website direction. This is the document to use with Pedro tomorrow before anyone gets lost in the full research file.

---

## 1. Meeting Goal

Agree the final Phase 3 direction for the HQC website:

1. Confirm the new website structure.
2. Confirm the three homepage routes.
3. Agree which pages we are writing and building first.
4. Review the key copy direction for each page.
5. Identify which claims Pedro must confirm before the copy goes live.

This meeting should not try to rewrite every word live. The job is to approve the direction, spot anything commercially wrong, and confirm what proof can be used.

---

## 2. The Core Decision

The website should stop leading with luxury/wellness/concierge language.

The new direction should be:

> Cleaning support for London homes, property teams, and premium spaces that need to stay ready.

This gives HQC a clearer commercial position:

- Property teams can see a reason to enquire.
- Homeowners can still see HQC as high-standard and trustworthy.
- Commercial and wellness spaces still have a route, but they no longer define the whole brand.

## 4. The Homepage In Plain English

The homepage should do five jobs quickly:

1. Say what HQC does.
2. Say who HQC serves.
3. Show why HQC can be trusted.
4. Let the visitor choose the right route.
5. Push the visitor toward quote/contact.

### Recommended H1

Cleaning services in London for homes, property teams and premium spaces

### Recommended supporting copy

High Quality Clean provides reliable cleaning for London homes, managed properties, offices, tenancy turnovers and post-renovation resets. We combine careful handling, clear scopes and high standards so every space is ready for the next visit, viewing, handover or day of use.

### Trust strip

Fully insured | Vetted team | Clear scopes | Quality checks | London and select Surrey coverage

Only use claims Pedro confirms.

## 5. The Three-Segment Section

This is the most important homepage section after the hero. It should not be a vague services grid. It should be a self-selection tool.

### Heading

Choose the cleaning support you need

### Intro

Different spaces need different cleaning decisions. Start with the route that best matches your property, team or home.

### Route 1: Property and Facilities Cleaning

For estate agents, lettings teams, landlords, property managers, block managers and developers who need properties ready for viewings, handovers, tenants, residents or sale.

Key services:

- void and post-tenancy cleaning
- block and communal area cleaning
- estate and lettings agent support
- developer handover cleaning
- portfolio cleaning support

CTA:

Explore Property and Facilities

### Route 2: Home Cleaning

For London homes that need dependable regular cleaning, deep cleaning, end-of-tenancy cleaning, move-out resets or careful cleaning after renovation work.

Key services:

- regular residential cleaning
- deep cleaning
- end of tenancy cleaning
- post-renovation cleaning
- carpet and upholstery add-ons

CTA:

Explore Home Cleaning

### Route 3: Commercial and Wellness Cleaning

For boutique offices, studios, wellness spaces and client-facing premises where cleaning affects staff, visitors and the customer experience.

Key services:

- office cleaning
- wellness and studio cleaning
- out-of-hours cleaning
- high-touch cleaning

CTA:

Explore Commercial Cleaning

## 7. Pages To Deprioritise

These should not lead the new website:

- Well-Clean
- Well-Clean Advisory
- Concierge Laundry
- Ironing and Laundry
- Specialist Cleaning as a vague category
- Wellness as the central brand idea

They can exist lower down if commercially needed, but they should not define the homepage.
