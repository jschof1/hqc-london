When to move each lead
01
New Lead
A new enquiry needs attention. Check the details and record who will
follow up.
02
Contacted
You have called or personally messaged the lead and recorded it. Log
the outcome; an automatic form reply does not count.
03
Scheduled
The booking is confirmed with the customer. Record the agreed date
and job details; a requested service date does not count.
04
Paid
Full payment has been received and confirmed. A deposit does not
count. A prepaid job still needs to be completed before a review
request.
05
Review Request
Sent
The service is completed and a review request has actually been
sent. Record the send; payment or a review posted by the customer
does not count.

FROM ENQUIRY TO FOLLOW-UP
The daily routine
Keep leads and bookings moving with a few simple habits.
GHL > Opportunities > Lead Management. Read the contact's Notes and your assigned Tasks.
1
Review new enquiries
Read the latest contact note and open your assigned task.
2
Contact and record
Call or personally message the lead. Log the attempt and move to Contacted.
3
Set the next action
Add a dated follow-up task. Move to Scheduled when the customer confirms the booking.
4
After the job
Confirm full payment. Request a review only after completed service, then record the actual send.
