# HQC Phase 3 Positioning and Copy Brief

Date: 20 May 2026

## Where We Are

Phase 1 is strong enough to act on. The site has a visibility problem and a click-choice problem: 371 clicks from 35,725 impressions over 16 months, site-wide CTR around 1.04%, with non-branded CTR at roughly 0.37%. The homepage has most of the visibility, while service and area pages underperform.

Phase 2 is directionally right but not yet copy-ready. The strongest decision so far is moving away from "luxury concierge" as the lead idea and toward a multi-segment cleaning partner model.

The three buyer segments should now drive the copy:

1. Property and Facilities
2. Homes
3. Commercial and Wellness

## Strategic Positioning

HQC should be positioned as:

> A detail-led cleaning partner for London homes, property teams, and premium spaces where reliability, presentation, and careful handling matter.

This is clearer than "luxury cleaning" because it says who it is for, what the work protects, and why the buyer should care.

## Core Message Rules

- Lead with the service and buyer situation before brand language.
- Use "premium" as proof through standards, not as decoration.
- Replace abstract wellness language with practical outcomes: cleaner handovers, fewer complaints, consistent attendance, safer handling of finishes, better presentation.
- Keep trust markers high on the page: insured, vetted, DBS-checked where true, director-led oversight, quality checks, clear communication, fixed or scoped quotes.
- Write for the person who is worried about reliability, standards, damage, deadlines, or having to manage the cleaner.

## Segment 1: Property and Facilities

Audience:
Estate agents, letting agents, property managers, block managers, developers, landlords with multiple properties, commercial site operators.

Primary pain:
They need properties cleaned, reset, or presented quickly without chasing unreliable suppliers.

Risks they want to avoid:
Missed handovers, tenant complaints, viewing delays, poor communal presentation, contractor dust left behind, repeated supplier management.

Message angle:
"One trusted cleaning partner for property presentation, tenancy turnover, and managed spaces."

Proof to request from Pedro:
Estate/letting agent relationships, repeat property work, before/after photos, response times, examples of urgent turnarounds, testimonials from property contacts.

Copy prompts:
- What property situations do we handle repeatedly?
- What happens when the clean is late, incomplete, or poorly communicated?
- What does HQC do that reduces workload for the property manager?
- What can we safely promise about turnaround, reporting, access, keys, and repeat work?

Priority pages:
- Property and Facilities hub
- Block Management Cleaning
- Estate and Lettings Agents
- Void and Post-Tenancy Cleaning
- Developer Handover Cleaning
- Amenity and Communal Spaces
- Partners Portfolio Programme

## Segment 2: Homes

Audience:
Upper-mid to high-end households, premium apartment occupiers, tenants, landlords, homeowners preparing for sale/viewing/occupation.

Primary pain:
They want a clean home without inconsistent standards, awkward supervision, or damage risk.

Risks they want to avoid:
Poor-quality cleaners, missed details, delicate finishes damaged, strangers rotating through the home, unclear scope, hidden extras.

Message angle:
"Reliable home cleaning for people who care about standards, detail, and trust."

Proof to request from Pedro:
Repeat residential clients, examples of first-clean-to-maintenance conversion, delicate surface experience, client language about trust and consistency.

Copy prompts:
- What do customers complain about before switching to HQC?
- What does a good first reset make possible?
- Which parts of a home do clients notice most when cleaners miss them?
- What do clients need to know before trusting someone with keys, access, or premium finishes?

Priority pages:
- Homes hub
- Regular Residential Cleaning
- End of Tenancy Cleaning
- End of Tenancy Cleaning Wandsworth
- Post-Renovation Cleaning
- Deep Cleaning
- Carpet and Upholstery

## Segment 3: Commercial and Wellness

Audience:
Boutique offices, studios, wellness/recovery spaces, client-facing commercial spaces.

Primary pain:
They need a consistently presentable environment without disruption to clients, staff, or operations.

Risks they want to avoid:
Poor first impressions, hygiene issues, unreliable attendance, weak security protocols, inconsistent washroom/kitchen standards.

Message angle:
"Consistent cleaning for client-facing spaces where presentation and trust affect the business."

Proof to request from Pedro:
Commercial clients, office/studio references, out-of-hours work, keyholding/security process, specific protocols for wellness environments.

Copy prompts:
- What would make a studio or office switch cleaning providers?
- What areas affect client perception most?
- What operational details matter: scheduling, access, consumables, reporting, consistency?
- What can we prove about reliability without sounding generic?

Priority pages:
- Commercial and Wellness hub
- Office Cleaning
- Office Cleaning Belgravia
- Office Cleaning Mayfair
- Wellness and Studio Cleaning

## Homepage Copy Direction

The homepage should not open with "Where profound care meets perfected detail."

Use something closer to:

> Cleaning services for London homes, property teams, and premium spaces.

Support line:

> High Quality Clean provides reliable, detail-led cleaning for regular homes, tenancy turnovers, post-renovation resets, managed properties, and client-facing commercial spaces.

The first screen should answer:

- What do you clean?
- Who do you clean for?
- Why are you reliable?
- What should I do next?

Recommended first CTA:
"Request a Cleaning Quote"

Recommended secondary CTA:
"Choose Your Service"

## Three-Segment Section

This should be a routing section, not a service catalogue.

Card 1: Property and Facilities
For estate agents, landlords, property managers, developers, blocks, and managed portfolios.
CTA: "View Property Services"

Card 2: Homes
For regular cleaning, move-out cleaning, deep cleans, and post-renovation resets.
CTA: "View Home Cleaning"

Card 3: Commercial and Wellness
For boutique offices, studios, wellness spaces, and client-facing premises.
CTA: "View Commercial Services"

Each card should include:

- who it is for
- the main problem
- 3 example services
- 1 trust proof
- 1 CTA

## Phase 2 Gaps Before Writing

Before final copy is locked, Pedro should answer these:

1. Which customer type has the highest priority for the next 12 months: property/facilities or residential?
2. Which claims are definitely true: DBS checked, insured, same-day response, fixed quote, director-led oversight, quality checks, keyholding?
3. What real examples prove property/facilities capability?
4. Which areas are commercially worth prioritising beyond the six visible ones?
5. Which services should be removed, folded in, or kept as secondary?
6. What offers or CTAs are actually viable: site visit, fixed quote, portfolio consultation, introductory rate?

## Copy Writing Prompt

Use this prompt when drafting each page:

> Write copy for the [PAGE NAME] page for High Quality Clean. The target buyer is [BUYER]. Their main problem is [PAIN]. Their risk is [RISK]. The desired outcome is [OUTCOME]. The tone should be clear, premium, practical, and trust-led, without sounding corporate or poetic. Lead with the service and buyer situation. Include operational proof, clear CTAs, and plain-English section headings. Avoid vague luxury language unless it is supported by specific standards, careful handling, quality control, or trust proof.
