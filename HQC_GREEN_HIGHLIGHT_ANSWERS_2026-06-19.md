## 17. Website Offer Hook

Green point:
- Current wording: "Lock in your 30% introductory rate today and experience the HQC difference."
- Problem: some customers take the discount then cancel quickly.
- Pedro wants a revised version that keeps the offer attractive but positions it as a loyalty-based recurring-client rate.
- Needs to be clear but not too harsh or legalistic.

Recommended structure:

Headline:

> Join Hundreds of Satisfied Clients

Offer wording:

> Claim Your 30% HQC Introductory Cleaning Offer

Buttons:
- Claim My Home Cleaning Offer
- Claim My Commercial Cleaning Offer

Small print:

> Limited availability | New recurring clients only | 12-month loyalty rate available | No card needed to enquire | Offer confirmed after enquiry

Recommended condition wording:

> The introductory rate is designed for new recurring clients. If the recurring agreement is cancelled within the first 3 months, the loyalty discount may be recalculated at the standard rate in line with the agreed service terms.

Why this is better:
- It protects HQC.
- It is softer than "the difference will become payable".
- It avoids making the first screen feel punitive.
- It still makes clear that the offer is for recurring clients, not one-off discount seekers.

Testing checklist:
- Does the offer still encourage enquiries?
- Is the 12-month recurring relationship clear?
- Is the cancellation condition clear without sounding harsh?
- Does it work on desktop and mobile?
- Are the buttons and small print readable?
