## 30 Percent Offer Recommendation

Pedro's current proposed wording:
- Headline: Join Hundreds of Satisfied Clients
- Offer: Claim Your 30% HQC Introductory Cleaning Offer
- Small print: Limited availability | New recurring clients only | 12-month loyalty rate available | No card needed to enquire | Offer confirmed after enquiry
- Extra condition: if cancelled within first 3 months, discount already applied is recalculated at standard rate and difference becomes payable.

Issue:
The extra condition is commercially sensible but may feel harsh if placed too prominently.

Recommended public version:

Headline:
Join Hundreds of Satisfied Clients

Offer wording:
Claim Your 30% HQC Introductory Cleaning Offer

Buttons:
- Claim My Home Cleaning Offer
- Claim My Commercial Cleaning Offer

Small print:
Limited availability | New recurring clients only | 12-month loyalty rate available | No card needed to enquire | Offer confirmed after enquiry

Condition wording:
The introductory rate is designed for new recurring clients. If the recurring agreement is cancelled within the first 3 months, the loyalty discount may be recalculated at the standard rate in line with the agreed service terms.

Why:
This keeps the commercial protection but sounds less punitive than "the difference will become payable" in the first screen.
