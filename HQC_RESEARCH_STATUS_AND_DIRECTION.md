# HQC Research Status and Direction

Date: 20 May 2026

Purpose: reconcile the Drive research, SEO findings, competitor assessment, and Phase 3 copy direction.

## Honest Status

The previous copy draft was directionally aligned, but it understated one major point from the Drive document: Property and Facilities is not merely one of three equal audience routes. It is the top strategic revenue opportunity and should lead the next website structure.

The website direction is now clear:

> Move away from luxury concierge/service-catalogue positioning and become a multi-segment cleaning partner organised around three buyer groups, led by Property and Facilities.

## What Has Been Completed

### Phase 1: Diagnosis and Evidence

This is substantially complete.

Key findings from the Drive document:

- 371 clicks from 35,725 impressions over 16 months.
- Site-wide CTR: 1.04%.
- Non-branded CTR: 0.37%.
- Branded CTR: 8.0%.
- Homepage dominates visibility with 27,767 impressions, around 91% of total visibility.
- Average UK position is roughly 30, which means page 3.
- UK is the only commercially relevant market in the data.
- Mobile converts 2.6x better than desktop but receives only a third of impressions.
- Technical/canonical work has already reduced 70 indexed URL variants to 38 actual topic pages.

Interpretation:

The site is being found, but not chosen. The primary issue is no longer technical drag; it is content, intent matching, search snippets, and conversion clarity.

### Phase 2: Customer Pain and Keyword Direction

This is partly complete and directionally strong, but still has proof gaps.

The confirmed top customer groups are:

1. Property and facilities decision-makers
2. Premium residential clients
3. Boutique commercial and wellness operators

The confirmed top services to grow are:

1. Regular residential cleaning
2. Move in / move out / end of tenancy cleaning
3. Post-renovation / after-builders / fine-finish cleaning

The confirmed customer problems are:

1. "My last cleaners were inconsistent or did not do a good enough job."
2. "I need this property ready quickly for moving, handover, viewing, sale, or occupation."
3. "I do not want damage to valuables, delicate finishes, marble, high ceilings, or expensive furnishings."
4. "I want a cleaner or team I can rely on without having to manage everything myself."
5. "I need a proper reset after building works, neglect, or before regular maintenance can begin."

Interpretation:

This is enough to start Phase 3 positioning. However, before final page copy is locked, Pedro still needs to confirm proof points: real client examples, strongest claims, priority areas, and which services should be removed or folded in.

## Competitor Lessons

### Total Clean

Main lesson: operational trust and scale signals convert.

HQC should borrow:

- plain service labels
- visible trust markers
- sectors served
- scheduling reliability
- clear operational benefits
- broader page architecture

### Complete Clean Management

Main lesson: practical pain-led copy works.

HQC should borrow:

- clearer answers to real buyer concerns
- direct references to poor standards, slow response, inconsistent cleaners
- sector/local SEO coverage
- consistency, accountability, efficiency, and reliability language

### Maid2Clean

Main lesson: reduce thinking effort.

HQC should borrow:

- fast self-selection
- area/service routing
- direct enquiry path
- simple trust language

## Current Strategic Direction

The site should become:

> A London-first cleaning partner for property teams, homes, and selected premium commercial spaces.

More specifically:

> Cleaning support for London homes, property teams, and premium spaces that need to stay ready.

This is better than "luxury cleaning" because:

- it names the audience
- it names the category
- it implies an operational outcome
- it supports SEO service clusters
- it gives Pedro room to sell premium standards without sounding decorative

## Correct Three-Segment Hierarchy

### Segment 1: Property and Facilities

This is the lead segment.

Audience:
Estate agents, lettings agents, property managers, block managers, developers, landlords with multiple properties, commercial site operators.

Lead message:
One trusted partner across a portfolio.

Why:
Largest revenue opportunity, currently zero meaningful site presence, competitors already own this language.

Priority pages:

1. Property and Facilities Hub
2. Property Management Cleaning
3. Block Management Cleaning
4. Estate and Lettings Agents
5. Void and Post-Tenancy Cleaning
6. Developer Handover Cleaning
7. Amenity and Communal Spaces
8. Partners Portfolio Programme

### Segment 2: Homes

This is the current revenue base.

Audience:
Upper-mid to high-end households who value quality, including homeowners, tenants, landlords, premium apartment occupiers, and people preparing a property for sale, viewing, or occupation.

Lead message:
Reliable home cleaning for people who care about standards, detail, and trust.

Priority pages:

1. Homes Hub
2. Regular Cleaning
3. End of Tenancy Cleaning
4. End of Tenancy Cleaning Wandsworth
5. Post-Renovation Cleaning
6. Deep Cleaning
7. Carpet and Upholstery
8. Six priority areas: Belgravia, Knightsbridge, Kensington, Chelsea, Mayfair, Wandsworth

### Segment 3: Commercial and Wellness

This is useful but narrower.

Audience:
Wellness studios, boutique gyms, smaller premium offices, client-facing commercial spaces.

Lead message:
Premium standards and detail, supported by operational reliability and trust.

Priority pages:

1. Commercial and Wellness Hub
2. Office Cleaning
3. Office Cleaning Belgravia
4. Office Cleaning Mayfair
5. Wellness and Studio Cleaning

## Implication for the Copy Draft

The current Phase 3 copy draft is broadly aligned, but should be adjusted in one important way:

- The homepage should still let visitors self-select into three routes.
- But Property and Facilities should be treated as the primary strategic route, not visually or structurally buried.
- The first new page copy should be Property and Facilities Hub, not Homes.
- Homes remains essential, but it is refinement of the current revenue base rather than the biggest new opportunity.
- Commercial and Wellness should be secondary and proof-led.

The next copy deliverable should be:

## Bottom Line

The direction is not simply "make the site clearer."

The more precise direction is:

> Reposition HQC from a luxury/wellness-led cleaning brand into a London-first cleaning partner for property teams, high-standard homes, and selected premium commercial spaces, with Property and Facilities as the biggest strategic growth route.
